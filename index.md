# Polyhouse equipment quotation checklist

Use this to check a quotation for polyhouse or greenhouse equipment before you place an order. It applies to any supplier.

1. **Scope in writing.** Every item has a name, size, quantity and unit. Nothing is "as required".
2. **Structure.** Area, bay size, span, column and foundation specification, and whether erection is included.
3. **Covering and nets.** Film thickness and width, net mesh or shade factor, roll lengths, and the roll count against the roof and side area.
4. **Fixing hardware.** Wire clamps, springs, channels and profiles listed by type and size, matched to the film and frame.
5. **Irrigation and fertigation.** Emitter type and flow, laterals, filters, injector, and the pressure the pump must supply.
6. **Packing and dispatch.** How lines are packed so each can be counted on arrival, lead time, MOQ and delivery point.
7. **Subsidy.** If the project is under a subsidy scheme, ask which items fall under the approved cost norm. Check the current scheme documents; norms change.
8. **Terms.** Validity of the quote, payment schedule, warranty, and who handles claims.
